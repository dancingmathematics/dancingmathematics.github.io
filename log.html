Working record

Documentation log

A dated record of talks, workshops, fieldwork, writing, and performance. This is where the portfolio actually accumulates — on a site I control, with no algorithm deciding who sees it and no platform holding the archive.

Newest first. Each entry is a small unit of work: what happened, where, and what I took from it. Some entries are a sentence; some are a few paragraphs with a photograph. Both are fine — the point is that the record exists and stays under my control.

June 2026 Performance

Yerba Buena Gardens Festival

Performed Makepung, Kebyar Santi, and Joged Jegog with Gamelan Sekar Jaya at the Yerba Buena Gardens Festival in San Francisco. Outdoor stage, large and mostly unfamiliar audience — a useful reminder of how much of the spatial work in these dances is done in relation to a stage geometry that changes at every venue.

28 April 2026 Invited lecture

From dance movement to geometric reasoning

Invited lecture at the Department of Mathematics Education, Universitas Pattimura, Maluku: Multimodal analysis to trace learning pathways in embodied mathematics design. Focused on the analytic side rather than the design side — what it actually takes to trace a learning pathway across video, gesture, and gaze data.

23 April 2026 Colloquium

Heteroperception in joint action — SESAME Colloquium

Presented with Dor Abrahamson, Catalina Lomos, Sofia Hilkey, and Alik Palatnik at the SESAME Colloquium, UC Berkeley, on how collaborating learners break down and reconcile differing views of the same mathematical object.

17 April 2026 Outreach

Intersession at Cathedral School for Boys

Ran a session on designing the GRiD learning intervention for students at Cathedral School for Boys, San Francisco, as part of “SF’s influence on American consciousness — Intersession 2026”. Talking about research design with school-age students clarifies which parts of the argument are load-bearing.

March 2026 Conference

Flooring dance — EARLI SIGs 10, 17, 21 & 25, Augsburg

Single paper at the joint EARLI SIG meeting, University of Augsburg: From perception to inscription in a culturally situated embodied geometry design. The move from perception to inscription is the hinge of the whole dissertation, and this was the first time I presented it as such to a European audience.

2026 Publication

Two articles out

The emergence of sign–tool hybridity in situated geometry practice appeared in Educational Studies in Mathematics, and Dancing geometry in the International Journal of Mathematical Education in Science and Technology. Between them they cover the two halves of the argument: what the tool becomes, and how the line arrives.

May 2026 Milestone

Dissertation filed

Grounding geometry in culturally authentic movement practices: From attentional anchors to auxiliary lines in Balinese Dance — Ph.D. in Education, Learning Science and Human Development, University of California, Berkeley, supervised by Professor Dor Abrahamson.

July 2025 Workshop

Bridges Family Day & the Copernicus Science Center

Facilitated “Dancing geometry” at the Bridges Mathematics and Art Family Day in Eindhoven, then ran a workshop for educators and researchers at the Tinkatorium Station of the Copernicus Science Center in Warsaw. Two very different rooms; the same activity held in both, which is a reasonable first test of whether a culturally situated design can travel without being emptied out.

How to add an entry. Open log.html, copy the commented template at the top of the list, paste it directly above the newest entry, and fill it in. Set data-cat to one of: talk, workshop, fieldwork, writing, performance, teaching — the filter buttons work automatically. Photographs go in assets/img/log/.

A privacy habit worth keeping. Before publishing an image, check that no research participant, and no child, is identifiable unless your consent forms explicitly cover publication on a personal website. When in doubt, describe the moment in words instead. The log is valuable because it is a considered record, not a feed.
